# Product Scope

## What We're Building

**CrewLodge** - An AI-powered SaaS platform for airline crew hotel management.

## The Problem

Airlines book thousands of crew hotel rooms daily. The current process is:

- Manual and fragmented (emails, phone calls, spreadsheets)
- Expensive (poor rate visibility, no optimization)
- Slow during disruptions (delays, diversions require immediate re-accommodation)
- Disconnected from crew scheduling and flight operations

When a flight delay hits, operations teams spend hours calling hotels, negotiating rates, and coordinating rooms for entire crews. This costs airlines millions in unnecessary spend and operational overhead.

## What We Solve

**Speed:** Automate hotel sourcing from hours to minutes during disruptions
**Cost:** Optimize bookings across cost, location, and crew requirements  
**Intelligence:** Centralize contracts, track rates, and provide real-time availability
**Automation:** Remove manual coordination with hotels and vendors

## Core Features

### 1. Contract Management

- Centralized database of all hotel agreements and negotiated rates
- Track SLAs, compliance, and contract terms
- Rate comparison across markets

### 2. Intelligent Sourcing

- AI-powered hotel selection based on:
  - Crew size and location
  - Rest requirement rules
  - Cost optimization
  - Distance to airport
  - Hotel availability and quality
- Automated outreach to hotels
- Real-time booking confirmation

### 3. Emergency Response

- Instant hotel sourcing during flight disruptions
- Multi-constraint optimization (cost + compliance + availability)
- Automated crew notification

### 4. Vendor Management

- Standardized communication with hotels
- Invoice parsing and rate verification
- SLA tracking and compliance monitoring

### 5. Analytics & Optimization

- Historical spend analysis by route/market
- Seasonal rate patterns
- Contract negotiation recommendations
- Disruption pattern forecasting

## How AI Is Used

AI is the optimization engine, not the product itself:

1. **Disruption Response:** Understands context (crew size, location, constraints), sources suitable hotels, ranks by multiple criteria, generates structured outreach
2. **Cost Optimization:** Analyzes historical patterns, forecasts demand, recommends contract adjustments, identifies arbitrage opportunities
3. **Automation:** Parses confirmations and invoices, detects rate discrepancies, automates follow-ups

## Success Metrics

- Average sourcing time during disruptions (target: <5 minutes)
- Cost per crew night vs baseline (target: 15-20% reduction)
- Emergency sourcing success rate (target: >95%)
- Manual coordination hours saved per month

## Target Users

- Airline operations teams (primary)
- Crew scheduling departments
- Procurement/vendor management
- Finance (cost analysis)

## What This Is NOT

- Not a generic travel booking platform
- Not a marketplace
- Not for consumer travel
- Not a broad GDS replacement

This is a vertical SaaS tool for one specific, high-value operational workflow in airlines.
